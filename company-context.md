# PMI Company Context (always loaded — do not fabricate beyond this)

## Company

Precisian Medical Instruments Inc. (PMI) — medical device startup. Delaware C-Corp, founded August 2025.
Flagship product: **VACTOR** — compact, 12 V (4x CR123A disposable), pocket-sized (188 mm x 40 mm, ~188 g) medical suction device for emergency medicine, military, and tactical use — designed for combat medics, paramedics, and EMS providers in prehospital/austere settings. Canister-free architecture built around a quick-swap pump head upstream of the fluid container, tactile PWM buttons (power/increase/decrease) operable with gloves, multi-color LED status indicators with a stealth mode, and a dust-tight/IP-rated housing (1 m water immersion, 30 min).
