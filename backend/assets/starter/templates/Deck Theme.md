# Deck Theme

Only lines of the form `key: value` under a `###` heading are read; everything else is ignored. Delete a line to keep the built-in value. Colours are 6-digit hex, type sizes are points, positions are inches. A bad value falls back to the default and is reported in the assistant's reply.

### Palette
background: 000000
card: 0A0A0A
primary: FFFFFF
body: C9C9C9
caption: 6F6F6F
accent: FF0000

### Fonts
display: Archivo
mono: JetBrains Mono

### Classifications
confidential_internal: CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL
confidential_proprietary: CONFIDENTIAL — PROPRIETARY INFORMATION
confidential_trade_secret: CONFIDENTIAL — TRADE SECRET
