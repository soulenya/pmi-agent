# Style Guide

These rules apply to every document the assistant writes, whatever the type. Templates in this folder decide structure; this doc decides wording and layout. Fonts and colours for Word documents and decks come from the design system chosen in Settings, so they do not belong here.

## Wording
- State facts and stop. No hype adjectives, no "this isn't X, it's Y", no re-explaining the mission.
- Short declarative sentences. One idea per paragraph.
- Numbers, dates, standards and part numbers exactly as recorded; never rounded or softened.
- The company is named in full on first use, then by its short name.

## Layout
- Title, then a one-line purpose statement, then the body.
- Headings in sentence case. No heading deeper than three levels.
- Tables for anything with more than two columns of facts; bullets otherwise.
- Every document ends with: who wrote it, the date, and the revision.

## Page header and footer
- Header right: document title · revision.
- Footer left: company short name · confidentiality marking when one applies.
